# Methodology

SIDSight is a small exploratory project designed to bring together selected country indicators and policy information relevant to Small Island Developing States.

The first version focuses on eight SIDS across the Pacific, Caribbean and Atlantic, Indian Ocean and South China Sea regions (AIS).

## Approach

The project uses a limited number of publicly available indicators that are intended to provide a simple starting point for comparing selected aspects:

* food import exposure;
* the role of agriculture, forestry and fisheries in the economy;
* digital connectivity;
* food insecurity; and
* data availability.

Where data are unavailable, this is shown explicitly rather than estimated.

## Data availability

The project reports the availability of four selected indicators for each country. This is used only to illustrate gaps in the selected dataset and should not be interpreted as a measure of country performance or statistical capacity.

## Country notes

Short country and policy notes are based on official or publicly available sources and are kept separate from the quantitative indicators.

## Use of digital tools

Digital tools are used to support data organization, research and visualization. Information included in the final project is checked against the underlying sources.

## Limitations

SIDSight is an exploratory portfolio project based on a deliberately small selection of countries and indicators. Cross-country comparisons should be interpreted with care because reporting years and data availability may differ.

The project does not produce official assessments, rankings or recommendations.
